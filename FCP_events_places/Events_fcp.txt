-5. Annual Events: 
    Santa Cruz Patron Saint Festival: The city’s main annual fair takes place from April 23 to May 3, honoring the town's patron saint with traditional festivities.

    Festival of the Souls and the Stars: Scheduled for November 2, 2026, this event blends the ancestral Hanal Pixán tradition with Maya astronomy and cosmology, featuring rituals, live music, and astronomical viewings.

    13th Xtaabay Festival: A cultural event held in October 2026 (specifically noted for October 11 in recent cycles) celebrating local heritage.

    La Diosa del Mar 2026: A cultural festival scheduled for July 10, 2026, focusing on marine and local cultural themes.

    177th Anniversary of the Caste War: Observed in late July 2026, commemorating a pivotal historical event in the region's Maya history.
